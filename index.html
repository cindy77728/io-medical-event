Recommended production controls

Important before connecting real inboxes

  • Single sign-on and least-privilege roles by Medical, Commercial, Compliance, Finance and Operations.
  • Approved email connector, encrypted storage, retention policy and immutable audit log.
  • Human verification of AI-extracted fields before creating or changing an event.
  • Cross-border, transfer-of-value, speaker contracting and local compliance rules by country.
  • Explicit prohibition and automatic detection of patient-identifiable information.
